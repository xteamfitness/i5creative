Common Questions

Questions we hear all the time.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most Phase 1 websites are completed in 2–4 weeks, depending on how quickly client content, feedback, and approvals are provided. We move fast — we just need you to respond when we need input.

What does a website from i5 Creative cost?

Every project is scoped individually based on your specific needs. What we can tell you: our pricing is designed for small and midsize businesses, not enterprise budgets. Request a free review and we'll give you a straight answer.

Do you work with businesses outside of Richmond, VA?

Yes. While we're based in Richmond, Virginia, we work with clients throughout the US. Our process is designed to work remotely without sacrificing quality or communication.

Will I be able to update my website myself?

That depends on the project. Some sites are built on CMS platforms for easy client editing; others are hand-coded for performance and precision. We'll recommend the right approach for your situation during the review conversation.

What happens after my website launches?

We offer ongoing support, and when you're ready, we can layer in lead capture, automated follow-up, CRM systems, and more. Your website is Phase 1 — there's a lot more your digital presence can do for you.
