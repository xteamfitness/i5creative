Our Story

A different kind of creative company.

After years of working with major entertainment, advertising, and technology clients in Los Angeles — and spending far too many hours sitting in I-5 traffic imagining a better way — we built a different kind of creative company.

One focused on clarity. Simplicity. Practical solutions. And helping businesses communicate more effectively.

We built i5 to do both — thoughtful design and real engineering capability.

Most creative firms specialize in either design or development. Most technology shops can build things but can't communicate. We built i5 to do both — and that philosophy has guided us since 1986.

Today, we help businesses in Richmond, Virginia and throughout the US modernize their online presence with the same level of thoughtful design and technical expertise typically associated with much larger firms.

Without the agency circus.

How We Work

Principles that guide every project.

01

Clarity over complexity.

We don't make things complicated to look impressive. Every decision — visual, structural, technical — should make things clearer and easier for your customers.

02

Design and engineering, together.

A beautiful site that doesn't work is a liability. A functional site that looks cheap destroys trust. We hold both to the same standard on every project.

03

No agency overhead.

You work directly with senior-level people. No account managers relaying messages. No layers of meetings and approvals. Just experienced professionals getting work done.

04

Practical over theoretical.

We don't build things to win awards. We build things that work — that generate inquiries, earn trust, and help your business grow. Form follows function, then both follow results.

05

Speed without shortcuts.

We move fast because we're experienced, not because we're cutting corners. A proper website doesn't have to take six months. It just has to be built by people who know what they're doing.

06

Built for growth.

Your website is Phase 1. We design everything with the next phase in mind — so when you're ready to add lead capture, automation, or CRM systems, the foundation is already solid.

Why i5

What sets us apart.

Most firms your size don't have access to the kind of design and engineering talent we bring to every project. Major agency clients typically require teams of 10+ and retainers in the six figures.

We built i5 specifically to bring that level of capability to small and midsize businesses — at a price point that makes sense, without the process overhead that inflates agency timelines and budgets.

The result: you get senior-level work, direct communication, and a finished product that genuinely reflects the quality of your business.
